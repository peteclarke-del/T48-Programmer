# Code of conduct

T48 Programmer welcomes careful technical contributions from people with
different levels of experience. Discussion should improve the evidence and the
software.

Be respectful, specific, and patient. Critique code and behaviour, not people.
Distinguish tested facts from assumptions, above all about pinouts and
voltages, where a confident guess costs someone a chip. Respect privacy,
copyright, and responsible disclosure. Accept decisions that keep an unproven
board or operation out of the application.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualised attention, deliberate
disruption, publication of private information, and sustained personal attacks
are unacceptable. Do not pressure anyone to publish ROM images or keys they do
not have permission to share.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
address on their GitHub profile. The maintainer may edit or remove
contributions, limit participation, suspend access, or ban a participant.
Reports will be handled confidentially.
